About Me

I am Md Abir Hassan Robin, currently pursuing a Master's degree in Flight Vehicle Design at Beihang University. My academic and research interests focus on the design and analysis of advanced flight vehicles, with particular emphasis on aerodynamics, computational fluid dynamics, aircraft overall design, and data-driven engineering methods.

My long-term research goal is to contribute to the development of next-generation aircraft through the integration of aerodynamic analysis, multidisciplinary design, computational simulation, and artificial intelligence.

Research Interests

Aircraft Design

Conceptual and overall aircraft design, multidisciplinary configuration development, and performance-oriented design.

Aerodynamics

Transonic aerodynamics, wing-flow analysis, aerodynamic performance, and computational investigation of flight vehicles.

Computational Fluid Dynamics

CFD simulation, numerical modeling, grid convergence, flow-field analysis, and aerodynamic prediction.

Data-Driven Aerodynamics

Surrogate modeling, Gaussian Process Regression, machine learning, and efficient prediction methods for aerospace applications.

AI for Aerospace Engineering

Integration of artificial intelligence with simulation, optimization, digital engineering, and next-generation aircraft development.

Advanced Flight Vehicles

Emerging aircraft configurations, VTOL concepts, intelligent aviation systems, and future air mobility.
